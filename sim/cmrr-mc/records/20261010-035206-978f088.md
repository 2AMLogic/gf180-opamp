## Method

- One ngspice process per sample (`.ac dec 20 0.1 1e+09`, 201 points): the testbench's dm drive (vinp +0.5 V, vinn -0.5 V AC) -> `op` -> `alter vcm acmag=1`, `alter vnac acmag=1` -> the same `.ac` with the cm drive (vinp = vinn = 1 V AC) -> `op`. The circuit is the systematic bench's (`sim/cmrr/testbench/tb_cmrr.spice`, line-for-line; guarded), with `sw_stat_mismatch` on its `.param` line.
- From the actual phasors of both sweeps, vd = v(vinp) - v(vinn), vc = (v(vinp) + v(vinn))/2, and `vout = Ad vd + Acm vc` is solved exactly per frequency (the systematic driver's solve, in the ngspice control language). The scalars are printed into each sample's log and read from it.
- **DC** = mean CMRR (dB) over 0.1-1 Hz (21 points); |Acm| and Ad at DC are band means. Spot values are exact grid samples (1 kHz, 10 kHz, 100 kHz, 1 MHz are sweep points; checked per sample). **f_u** = first falling 0 dB crossing of |Ad| by ngspice `meas` (linear interpolation in f; the systematic driver interpolates in log f -- the switch-off control bounds the difference).
- **Per-sample validity (blocking)**: every scalar present and finite; sweep grid as expected; dm excitation vd = 1, vc = 0 and cm excitation vc = 1 to 1e-06 V, residual |vd/vc| <= 1e-06; **dm/cm parity**: the DC output after the two sweeps agrees to 1 uV (the output carries the sampled offset, so a re-drawn device set would differ by mV); vinp = VCM; |vout - VCM| <= 0.1 V; Ad, CMRR and |Acm| flat over 0.1-1 Hz to 0.1 dB; f_u inside the sweep.
- **Population validity (blocking)**: every grid point contributes exactly N = 300 distinct sample indices, all valid; offset sigma > 1 mV at every point (mismatch visibly acts; klt's `family_mismatch` reports `active: null` for an `.include`d DUT, 2AMLogic/klayout-tools#2928).
