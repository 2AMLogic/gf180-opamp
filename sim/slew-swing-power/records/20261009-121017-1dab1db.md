## Extraction methods

- **Quiescent power** = `-i(Vdd) * VDD` at `Ibias = 10 uA` (first point of a one-step DC sweep, i.e. the operating point), follower at VCM, no load. `i(Vdd)` includes the bias reference branch (total supply power). Invalid if the supply current is not positive or does not exceed the bias branch current.
- **Slew rate**: follower, CL = 2 pF, input step 1 Vpp about VCM, rising edge at 1 us and falling edge at 3 us. Levels `vlo`/`vhi` = vout 10 ns before each edge (must equal vinp to 20 mV, else INVALID); slope = 0.6*(vhi-vlo)/(t80-t20) between the first 20% and 80% crossings (linear interpolation); reported = the SLOWER edge.
- **Output swing**: inverting unity-gain, `Vin` swept 0..3.63 V in 5 mV steps; swing = vout(upper) - vout(lower), where each edge is the output level at the FIRST of (a) |dvout/dvin| falling below 0.707 (-3 dB) x its mid-range value or (b) output device M6 (upper) / M7 (lower) leaving saturation (|Vds| < |Vdsat|), walking outward from VCM. Mid-range gain must be -1 +- 0.05; both devices must be saturated at VCM; a sweep that ends before either happens is INVALID.
