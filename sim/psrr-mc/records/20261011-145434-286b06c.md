### Near-cancellation knees (4 sample rails)

A draw whose supply term cancels the systematic one leaves the residual to the reactive path through the DUT's dominant pole: |Asupply| ~ sqrt(Re^2 + (k f)^2), flat below f_c = |Re|/k, rising toward +20 dB/dec above it. With f_c inside or near 0.1-1 Hz the band has NO plateau (no fixed dB tolerance covers it: f_c = 2.4 Hz alone gives ~1 dB) while every bench-quality check passes and the figure sits orders of magnitude above the numerical floor. The DC band is then accepted as a **near-cancellation knee**: the band profile must be monotonically non-decreasing (rising, single-knee, minimum at the 0.1 Hz edge); anything falling or humped is a defect, not a knee. The knee sample's DC figure is the 0.1 Hz asymptote, not the band mean. Diagnosed after the first full-grid attempt (PR #151) was blocked by its 0.1 dB plateau gate on 4 such draws and wrote no record; the 900-draw diagnostic (job `klt-sim-ba5466061590`) showed the profile: 7.7 dB monotone rise at |Avdd| = 1.9e-3 V/V, 1.3e7 x the floor.

## Method

- One ngspice process per sample (`.ac dec 20 0.1 1e+09`, 201 points): the testbench's dm drive (vinp +0.5 V, vinn -0.5 V AC, rails quiet) -> `op` -> `alter vcm acmag=0`, `alter vnac acmag=0`, `alter vdd acmag=1` -> the same `.ac` with 1 V AC on vdd -> `op` -> `alter vdd acmag=0`, `alter vss acmag=1` -> the same `.ac` with 1 V AC on the DUT's vss -> `op`. The circuit is the systematic bench's (`sim/psrr/testbench/tb_psrr.spice`, line-for-line; guarded), with `sw_stat_mismatch` on its `.param` line.
- Ad = v(vout) / (v(vinp) - v(vinn)) of the dm sweep and Asupply = v(vout) / v(rail) of each supply sweep, from the actual phasors, per frequency (the systematic driver's definitions, in the ngspice control language). |Asupply| below 1.45e-10 V/V is clamped elementwise in the deck. The scalars are printed into each sample's log and read from it.
- **DC** = the low-frequency plateau value per sample: the band mean over 0.1-1 Hz (21 points) for a flat band (the systematic record's definition); for a near-cancellation knee (non-flat band, monotonically rising profile -- see "Numerical floor and near-cancellation") the 0.1 Hz value, the f->0 asymptote. |Asupply| and Ad at DC follow the same rule. Spot values are exact grid samples (1 kHz, 10 kHz, 100 kHz, 1 MHz; checked per sample). **f_u** = first falling 0 dB crossing of |Ad| by ngspice `meas` (linear interpolation in f; the systematic driver interpolates in log f -- the switch-off control bounds the difference).
- **Per-sample validity (blocking)**: every scalar present and finite; sweep grid as expected; dm excitation vd = 1, vc = 0 and rails quiet to 1e-06 V; each supply sweep: driven rail = 1 V and the other rail quiet to 1e-06 V, both inputs quiet to 1e-09 V, input-leak term |Ad vd| below 1e-06 of the output; **dm/vdd/vss parity**: the DC output after each supply sweep agrees with the one after the dm sweep to 1 uV (the output carries the sampled offset, so a re-drawn device set would differ by mV); vinp = VCM; |vout - VCM| <= 0.1 V; each rail's DC band flat over 0.1-1 Hz to 0.1 dB or a near-cancellation knee (monotonically non-decreasing band profile; blocking in the invalid direction); f_u inside the sweep.
- **Population validity (blocking)**: every grid point contributes exactly N = 300 distinct sample indices, all valid; offset sigma > 1 mV at every point (mismatch visibly acts; klt's `family_mismatch` reports `active: null` for an `.include`d DUT, 2AMLogic/klayout-tools#2928).
