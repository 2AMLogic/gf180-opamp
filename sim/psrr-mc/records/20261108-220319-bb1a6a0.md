## Method

- ONE `klt sim` request over the whole grid (45 points x N = 300, `remote.hosts = 8` shards): one ngspice process per sample, the nominal record's three-sweep deck and per-sample validation, with `vinp` checked against the point's own commanded VCM = VDD/2 (which also proves the supply/VCM alters were applied). klt derives every per-sample seed from the one base seed over the whole request, so sharding changes no sample's value.
- Ad = v(vout) / (v(vinp) - v(vinn)) of the dm sweep and Asupply = v(vout) / v(rail) of each supply sweep, from the actual phasors, per frequency; |Asupply| below 1.45e-10 V/V is clamped elementwise in the deck. The scalars are printed into each sample's log and read from it. Spot values are exact grid samples; **f_u** = first falling 0 dB crossing of |Ad| by ngspice `meas`.
- **Per-sample validity (blocking)**: every scalar present and finite; sweep grid as expected; dm excitation vd = 1, vc = 0 and rails quiet to 1e-06 V; each supply sweep: driven rail = 1 V and the other rail quiet to 1e-06 V, both inputs quiet to 1e-09 V, input-leak term below 1e-06 of the output; **dm/vdd/vss parity**: the DC output after each supply sweep agrees with the one after the dm sweep to 1 uV (the three sweeps saw the same device draw); vinp = the point's VDD/2; |vout - VCM| <= 0.1 V; each rail's DC band flat over 0.1-1 Hz to 0.1 dB or a near-cancellation knee (monotonically non-decreasing band profile); f_u inside the sweep.
- **Population validity (blocking)**: every one of the 45 points contributes exactly N = 300 distinct sample indices, all valid; offset sigma > 1 mV at every point (mismatch visibly acts); floor/knee accounting of every point agrees with its samples.
