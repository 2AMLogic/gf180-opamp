## Conditions

- **Points**: the full ratified 45-point PVT grid -- 5 MOS corners x 3 temperatures x 3 supplies = 45 points.
- **Stimulus** (`tb_overdrive.spice`): the input rests at VCM; from 400 ns it is driven to VCM + 1.85 V (1 ns edge), which is above the VDD rail at every grid supply, saturating the output high for 600 ns; at 1000 ns the overdrive is removed (1 ns edge back to VCM); from 2600 ns the input is driven to VCM - 1.9 V, below ground at every grid supply, saturating the output low for 600 ns; at 3200 ns it is removed; run ends at 4900 ns; `.tran 5e-09 4.9e-06`.
- **Reference level**: v_ref = mean vout over the last 50 ns before the overdrive starts (the linear follower output; the systematic offset therefore does not enter).
- **Recovery band**: +-1% of |v_ref| (the issue's '+-1 % of the linear output').
- **Saturation requirement**: across the last 50 ns of each hold the output must have travelled >= 0.5 V in the overdriven direction and be essentially stopped -- residual velocity <= 0.5 V/us, ~1/30 of the nominal slew rate (the landing on the current-limited output-stage limits is soft: residual creep 0.06-0.12 V/us across the grid, and the negative clip limit sits ~0.5 V above ground, so a flatness or fraction-of-rail test would be meaningless); else the point is INVALID, never a fast recovery.
- **Latch**: a polarity is **not recovered** (and the point is flagged latched) when it is still outside the band at the end of the 1590 ns (high) / 1700 ns (low) recovery window, when it drifts by more than 0.1 bands across the final reference window (a slow tail), or when it settles more than one band from v_ref (hysteresis). A recovery time is the time from the removal edge to the LAST exit from the band, so a transient tolerance crossing that leaves the band again never counts as recovered.

## Method

- **Window vs small-signal GBW**: the ratified GBW >= 10 MHz gives a closed-loop time constant tau <= 1/(2 pi GBW) = 15.9 ns; the saturation hold is 600 ns = 38 tau (the compensation node and second stage settle into the saturated state) and each recovery window is >= 100 tau.
- Per point the reported recovery time is the WORSE of the high and low polarities; both polarities are in the table. Recovery is measured from the start of the 1 ns removal ramp.
- **Overshoot** is the furthest excursion past v_ref (mV, normalised to the v_sat -> v_ref travel); **preshoot** is a wrong-way first move deeper into saturation. Evidence only.
