## Conditions

- **Initial state** (`tb_enable.spice`): both 0..1 envelope sources start at 0, so at t = 0 the rail, the input (`v(vinp) = VCM_final * envelope`), the output and the bias are all at 0 (`.tran` without `uic`; the t = 0 solution is the trivial unpowered state). The extraction REJECTS a point whose first samples are not unpowered/unbiased (|v| <= 0.05 V on vdd, vinp, vout and the bias-mirror node, envelopes <= 0.02); the `powered-start` control below proves it does.
- **Final powered levels**: rail = the corner supply (2.97 / 3.30 / 3.63 V), input = VDD/2, bias = 10 uA, per point as listed in the tables; the traces end at those levels and the extraction requires the rail to reach the final value within 20 mV and the bias envelope to be fully on.
- **Steady reference v_ref**: a SEPARATE DC solution of the same bench with both enable envelopes fixed on (`reference/` below), one per point -- never read off the run being judged. It must be within 20 mV of the ideal input VDD/2, else the point is INVALID (invalid reference).
- **Diagnostic schedules** (every value below is an experiment condition, not a requirement):

| Scenario | Ramp | rail ramp (start, duration) | bias enable (start, duration) | bias disable / re-enable starts | events (ramps complete -> window end) | tstop | tstep |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| simultaneous | fast | 1 us, 100 ns | 1 us, 100 ns | none | enable: 1.1 -> 5.1 us | 5.1 us | 5 ns |
| simultaneous | slow | 1 us, 5000 ns | 1 us, 5000 ns | none | enable: 6 -> 10 us | 10 us | 10 ns |
| supply-first | fast | 1 us, 100 ns | 3.1 us, 100 ns | none | enable: 3.2 -> 7.2 us | 7.2 us | 5 ns |
| supply-first | slow | 1 us, 5000 ns | 8 us, 5000 ns | none | enable: 13 -> 17 us | 17 us | 10 ns |
| bias-cycle | fast | 1 us, 100 ns | 3.1 us, 100 ns | 7.2 us / 9.3 us | enable: 3.2 -> 7.2 us; re-enable: 9.4 -> 13.4 us | 13.4 us | 5 ns |
| bias-cycle | slow | 1 us, 5000 ns | 8 us, 5000 ns | 17 us / 24 us | enable: 13 -> 17 us; re-enable: 29 -> 33 us | 33 us | 10 ns |

  - `simultaneous`: rail and external bias enabled together (both ramps start at the same instant)
  - `supply-first`: rail ramps first; the bias is enabled GAP_T after the rail ramp completes
  - `bias-cycle`: rail ramps first, bias enabled GAP_T later (enable 1), then at fixed supply the bias is ramped back to 0 A, held off OFF_HOLD_T and re-enabled (enable 2 = the re-enable)
- **Acquisition**: time from the instant an event's LAST ramp completes to the last exit of |vout - v_ref| from a +-1% diagnostic band about v_ref (interpolated; negative = already in band before the ramps finished). It is claimed only when the output then STAYS in the band to the end of the window, drifts by <= 0.1 bands across the final 200 ns, and the in-band tail lasts >= 10% of the window -- **no recovery is inferred from a single tolerance crossing**. Otherwise the event is CENSORED and shown as **not acquired** (ranked worst, never given a finite time).
- **Window vs small-signal GBW**: the ratified GBW >= 10 MHz gives tau <= 15.9 ns; each observation window is 251 tau.
- **Unresolved points** are re-run over a 10x longer window before any persistent-failure statement (section below).
