## Extraction validation

Every sample was required to have finite `vout_v`/`vinp_v`/`vos_v`; `vinp` = the point's commanded VCM (VDD/2 from the grid axes, within 1e-06 V -- this also proves klt applied the supply/VCM alters); `vos_v = v(vout)-v(vinp)` to agree with `vout_v - vinp_v` within 1e-05 V; and |offset| < 0.25 V. Every one of the 45 points had to contribute exactly N=300 samples with distinct indices, and a nonzero sigma.

Result: all 13500 samples valid; driver statistics agree with klt's own per-point rollup where it reported one.
