# layout/first_stage — the amplifier's first stage as a routed sub-block (issue #328)

**Scope: a routed, labelled SUB-BLOCK — the five-device first stage only
(XM1–XM5 from `design/netlist/opamp_two_stage.spice`). This is NOT block
layout, NOT the full core (#241 keeps XM6/XM7/XCC/XRZ, the MIM
plate-orientation decision and the metal-stack decision record), NOT ERC,
parasitic extraction or post-layout simulation (#241, #279), and it makes **no
area claim and no T1 claim**. The 26.62 × 15.63 µm footprint below is the
routed bounding box of three generated cells plus their yard lanes, not a
ratified or measured block area. `manifests/gf180-opamp.json` is not changed
by this directory.**

Everything here is regenerated by one command from a clean checkout; no cell
was edited by hand.

## What is placed and routed, and why this way

`tools/first_stage.py` reads the five instance cards from the schematic and
generates three matched groups (`klt gen`, sizes derived from the cards —
nothing typed in):

| Group | Schematic | Generator and params (sizes = W/nf from the card) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `in` | XM1, XM2 (`nfet_03v3` L=1u W=3.6u nf=2) | `diff_pair` w=1.8 l=1 splits=2, guard ring, `pitch_x_um` 2.9 | common-centroid cross-quad: XM1 and XM2 each contribute one sub-instance to every column, so the two input legs interleave diagonally — the standard matched-pair style for an input pair |
| `load` | XM3, XM4 (`pfet_03v3` L=1u W=6u nf=2) | `diff_pair` w=3.0 l=1 splits=2, `mirror: true`, guard ring (draws the n-well tie), `pitch_x_um` 2.9 | same cross-quad for the mirror load; `mirror` labels the legs M1/M2 |
| `tail` | XM5 (`nfet_03v3` L=2u W=6u nf=2) | `mos_array` w=3.0 l=2 fingers=2, guard ring | the pilot's existing `w_um = W/nf` + `fingers = nf` mapping (`layout/pilot/tools/pilot.py`), which already LVS-matches on `pilot_nfet_mb1_m5` |

The `w_um = W/nf` + `splits = nf` mapping draws `nf` parallel unit strips per
leg — the same split the pilot uses for `mos_array` fingers, relying on the
LVS request's `combine_devices: true` + `compare_parameters` (W, L) to fold
the parallel sub-instances back to the schematic's total W. The negative
control below proves that compare is live on this composed cell.

**Column pitch.** Both `diff_pair`s widen their inter-unit column pitch by
0.5 µm over the generator's own legal minimum (2.4 µm at L=1 µm, read from a
scratch probe; `drc_hints.pitch` in each `gen` envelope records
`col_pitch_um: 2.9`, `min_col_pitch_um: 2.4`, `pitch_x_clamped: false`). At
the default pitch two different nets' router fixtures share the inter-unit
gap on every row — the west unit's drain stub and via pad facing east, the
east unit's source facing west — 0.19 µm apart where the deck demands 0.28,
which is what made the placed-only slice of PR #349 unroutable
([klayout-tools#5764](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/5764),
fixed by `params.pitch_x_um` in klt#6242). The margin is a routing
clearance, not a device dimension: the pad edges sit (margin − 0.16) µm
apart, so 0.28 needs at least 0.44, and 0.5 is the value klt's own
regression for #5764 uses. W, L and splits stay netlist-derived and the
cross-quad interleave is unchanged by the pitch.

**Routing** (`klt gen-compose`, explicit legs on per-net planes, width
0.28 µm = the deck floor). gf180mcuD 5LM: metal2 = 36, metal3 = 42,
metal4 = 46.

| Net | Terminals | Plane | Route |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vdd` | XM3/XM4 sources, `load` n-well tap | metal2 | inside the load block: both gate-row dodge lanes, plus a south yard lane to the ring's `TAP_S` |
| `vss` | XM5 source, `in` and `tail` substrate taps | metal3 | south yard lane tying the two nfet rings to XM5's source |
| `vinp`, `vinn` | the input-pair gates | metal3, metal4 | one gate lane each between the pair's two gate rows |
| `tail` | the four input-pair sources, XM5 drain | metal2 | west bus on the west column's sources, south yard lane to the east column, a bus up the east column's own source-stub column, then a dodge lane over row 2 and through the inter-block channel into XM5's drain |
| `n1` | XM1 drains, XM3 drain, **all four pfet gates** | metal4 | south yard lane from XM1's west drain, a gate bus inside the load (both row-1 gates joined along their row, both columns joined by the gate-stub columns), north yard lane to XM1's east drain |
| `n2` | XM2 drains, XM4 drains | metal3 | stays north of the input pair: a loop west and north of the load, the north yard lane, and a climb through the `load`/`in` channel |
| `ibias` | XM5 gate | — | single-terminal in this cut; labelled at its port, no route |

`n1` carries **all four** pfet gates: in the schematic `XM4 n2 n1 vdd vdd`,
the mirror's output gate is `n1` like the diode-connected XM3's. The
declare-only connectivity of PR #349 left both M2 gates off every net; the
routed cell exposes that as two floating gate nets, so it is fixed here.

**What this placement establishes: input-pair matching and mirror-load
matching only.** The bias mirror `XMB1/XM5/XM7` is cut by this slice — XM5's
placement is a lone tail device, NOT a matched mirror element; do not read it
as one (#241 places that group).

The sub-netlist (`ref/first_stage.klt.spice`) is cut mechanically from the
schematic — the five instance cards verbatim, continuation lines included —
with `.subckt first_stage vdd vss vinp vinn tail n1 n2 ibias`. All eight nets
are ports of that `.subckt`; extraction and LVS both receive the same eight
names as the declared pin set, so the labelled pins and the port list agree
exactly (`results.json` → `extract.pins`).

Nothing is installed on the host; no ngspice is run. The pin was bumped from
`klt 0.8.0` deliberately and together with `layout/pilot/regen.sh`, because
the routing fix lands only in klt builds after 0.9.2; when a release carries
it, bump both directories together again and re-verify the numbers here.
`layout/output_stage/` still pins `klt 0.8.0` under its own issue and is not
touched by this directory.

Reproducibility, as measured in this directory at the pin above: two
consecutive runs of `regen.sh` from a clean checkout produce **byte-identical
GDS files and a byte-identical `results.json`** (klt zeroes the GDS timestamp
records, so there is no wall-clock content in the layout). The one moving
field is `evidence/first_stage.extract.klt.json`'s `timing.*` block
(wall-clock durations — scheduler noise, not content). The composed GDS's
sha256 is
`19303294b03add3ef743ef4bf04e48bbe5249a4e06c7487cfc32e5259f74a953`
across runs.

No cell is edited by hand — the mechanical form: `tools/first_stage.py`
asserts that the checked layout is the layout this run produced, against
**every** envelope. The DRC envelope carries the checked layout's content hash
as `provenance.input.content_hash` (`role: "layout"`) and the LVS and
negative-control envelopes as `environment.layout_sha256`; all three equal
`results.json` → `routed.layout_sha256` and the sha256 of the committed
`gds/first_stage.gds`. (The DRC/LVS difference is key shape only — the `drc`
envelope has no `environment` block, not a missing hash.)

## Results (actual runs; `results.json` and `evidence/` hold the raw output)

| Check | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Routed composition | `first_stage` cell, placement bbox 24.16 × 11.55 µm, routed bbox 26.62 × 15.63 µm (not an area claim); `unrouted_nets: []`, 7 nets routed, 8 pin labels |
| DRC, klt curated deck | **clean** (0 violations), 59 rules checked / 68 skipped, deck `sha256:778e9a4eba34b67a0427367917b4c9c2602d44ff085d663affd302a8d0f197cd` (127 rules) |
| Extract | 6 nfet + 4 pfet — all ten schematic instances (XM1–XM5, nf-expanded) recognised; 8 pins |
| LVS vs the cut sub-netlist | **match**, `error_count: 0` — 8/8 nets, 5/5 devices, 8/8 pins |
| Negative control (XM1 `W` 3.6u → 7.2u in the reference, GDS untouched) | **mismatch**, 1 `device.property` error, layout hash equal to the positive run's — tripped |

- "DRC, klt curated" is klt's curated `gf180mcu` deck only (a subset of the
  foundry rules; antenna and density are not covered), so `clean` is not
  foundry DRC signoff. The curated deck at this pin **does** carry
  `contact.ongrid.1`, `sab.separation.contact.1` (SB.4) and
  `poly2.enclosing.sab.1` (SB.10) — the three rules the pilot README's
  historical note called blind spots at klt 0.7.0 — plus `*.ongrid.1` for
  every drawn layer; that stale warning has been struck from
  `layout/pilot/README.md`. (The PDK runset itself remains unavailable on
  these workers, #282.) The four `layers_in_stream_without_rules`
  (34/10, 36/10, 42/10, 46/10) are the pin-label layers.
- The LVS gate is `status == "match"` and `error_count == 0`, never
  `mismatch_count`: the positive run reports `mismatch_count: 1`, a
  `topology` *warning* ("device class has no counterpart … 'bjt'"), which is
  not an error.
- The negative control perturbs XM1's `W` in a copy of the reference only and
  re-compares the **same** GDS: `device.property: 1` proves the (W, L)
  compare sees the diff_pair's splits-folded width (two 1.8 µm units folded
  to the schematic's single `W=3.6u`), not just its presence.
- Every generator-drawn guard ring/tap and every label comes from the
  generator and the compose request — nothing is hand-drawn. The guard
  rings' taps are tied into their nets by routing (`vdd` at the load's
  n-well tap, `vss` at both nfet rings' substrate taps), so the extracted
  bulk terminals sit on the same nets as the schematic's fourth nodes.
- Which AC-2 claims rest on a mechanical check and which on inspection: the
  pin labels are mechanical (`routed.pins_labelled` is asserted to be the
  eight subckt ports, and the extracted pin list is asserted equal to the
  declared set); the matched-pair style is a property of the generator's
  cross-quad (asserted by klt's own tests at the widened pitch); the
  guard-ring/tap claim rests on the generators' own geometry plus the
  composed LVS match — `klt ring-check` is not expressible against the
  composed cell because the devices' active/metal share the rings' layers
  (merging them is not an annulus), and `klt tap-distance` has no per-block
  subject/tap split; neither is claimed as a pass.

## Tool limitations met on the way (filed on klayout-tools, described generically)

- [#5764](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/5764) — the
  `diff_pair` inter-unit pad pitch that blocked the routed slice of PR #349.
  **Closed** by `params.pitch_x_um` (klt#6242); the composition uses it.
- [#5765](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/5765) —
  `klt layout-plan execute` cannot wire a `splits >= 2` diff_pair (the
  subckt-call digest has no per-instance device to declare). **Closed** by
  making that mis-declaration an explicit error (klt#6078); the verb for this
  composition remains `klt gen-compose` with explicit connectivity, sizes
  still netlist-derived. `layout-plan execute` was tried first at 0.8.0.
- [#7501](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/7501) — a leg
  whose backbone turns within (pad half + deck spacing + width/2) of its own
  via-drop pad composes `routed: true` and leaves a same-net notch only
  `klt drc` sees. Met here on vinn's gate lane: a turn 0.54 µm above the
  gate port passed the router's own check and drew one `metal4.space.1`.
  **Open**; avoided by keeping every turn at least 0.63 µm from its terminal
  (the lane constants in `build_routed_request`), with the composed DRC as
  the gate.
