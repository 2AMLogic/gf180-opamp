# layout/output_stage — the output stage + compensation as a routed sub-block (issue #424)

**Scope: a routed, labelled SUB-BLOCK — the five devices XM6, XM7, XMB1, XCC
and XRZ from `design/netlist/opamp_two_stage.spice` (the output half of the
core). This is NOT block layout, NOT the full core (#241 keeps joining the
two halves, the MIM plate-orientation decision and the metal-stack decision
record), NOT ERC, parasitic extraction or post-layout simulation (#279), and
it makes **no area claim and no T1 claim**. The 140.64 × 21.11 µm footprint
below is the placed+routed bounding box of five generated cells, not a
ratified or measured block area. `manifests/gf180-opamp.json` and
`manifests/gf180-opamp.signoff.json` are not changed by this directory.**

Everything here is regenerated by one command from a clean checkout; no cell
was edited by hand, no geometry was hand-drawn, and no spec value was changed.

## What is composed, and why this way

`tools/output_stage.py` reads the five instance cards from the schematic and
generates five blocks (`klt gen`, sizes derived from the cards — nothing
typed in), all with the pilot's established generator mappings
(`layout/pilot/`, issue #77):

| Block | Schematic | Generator and params (sizes from the card) | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `m6` | XM6 (`pfet_03v3` L=1u W=72u nf=24) | `mos_array` w=3 l=1 fingers=24, gate contact, guard ring | the PMOS output device |
| `mb1` | XMB1 (`nfet_03v3` L=2u W=6u nf=2) | `mos_array` w=3 l=2 fingers=2, gate contact, guard ring | the bias diode |
| `m7` | XM7 (`nfet_03v3` L=2u W=36u nf=12) | `mos_array` w=3 l=2 fingers=12, gate contact, guard ring | the NMOS output device |
| `cc` | XCC (`cap_mim_2f0_m4m5_noshield` 16.8u × 16.8u) | `cap_array` plate 16.8 × 16.8, num=1 | Miller compensation |
| `rz` | XRZ (`ppolyf_u_1k` r_width=2u r_length=22u) | `res_array` flavor 1k, length 22, width 2, num=1 | the series Rz |

**Placement, and its reason.** `klt gen-compose` places the five blocks in
one row, `m6 | mb1 | m7 | cc | rz`, 4 µm channels, all same-orientation.
XMB1 and XM7 share L=2u and W/nf=3u (a 1:6 width ratio) and are placed
**adjacent, as one matched group** — this slice's only matching claim:
**XMB1:XM7**. XM5, the third leg of that bias mirror, is placed alone in
`layout/first_stage/` and is deliberately NOT claimed as matched here;
matching of the full `XMB1/XM5/XM7` group stays with #241. The 4 µm channels
carry the routing lanes (below) clear of the deck's 0.28 µm metal spacing
and the MIM generator's 1.2 µm foreign-clearance hint.

**MIM orientation is inherited, not decided.** XCC's card puts `n2` on pin 1
and `nz` on pin 2; klt extracts the plates as (BOT, TOP), so BOT = `n2` and
TOP = `nz`, exactly as in `pilot_mim_cc`. `layout/pilot/README.md` flags that
this puts the high-impedance node `n2` on the bottom plate — the plate with
unmodelled substrate parasitics — and calls orientation a placement-time
decision. **That decision belongs to #241; this slice inherits the card's
assignment unchanged.**

**Routing.** The six nets are declared as explicit composition connectivity
(the issue's connection table) and drawn by `klt gen-compose`'s explicit-leg
router on per-net planes: `vdd`/`vss` metal2 (two south yard lanes),
`ibias`/`vout` metal3, `n2` metal4 (into the MIM bottom plate, itself
metal4), `nz` top_metal (over the MIM top plate, itself metal5). Every
coordinate of every leg derives from the placed response's port positions
and bboxes; the lane offsets are clearance constants against the deck's
0.28 µm same-layer spacing. Result: **`unrouted_nets: []`, all six nets
status `routed`** (route lengths in `results.json` → `routed.nets`) — unlike
the first stage (#328), whose `diff_pair` pad-ladder spacing is blocked on
klayout-tools [#5764](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/5764).
This slice uses no `diff_pair`; its single-pad `mos_array`/`cap_array`/
`res_array` ports route cleanly under the same router.

The sub-netlist (`ref/output_stage.klt.spice`) is cut mechanically from the
schematic — the five instance cards verbatim, continuation lines included —
with `.subckt output_stage n2 vout ibias vdd vss` (`nz` internal).

## Regenerate (one command, from a clean checkout)

```
layout/output_stage/regen.sh
```

Same identity gate as `layout/pilot/regen.sh` and
`layout/first_stage/regen.sh`; refuses to run on any mismatch, then rebuilds
`gds/ ref/ extracted/ evidence/` and `results.json`. **No pin is bumped by
this directory** (AC: do not bump any pin in this issue).

| Item | Pinned identity |
|---|---|
| klayout-tools | `klt 0.8.0` (release; throwaway env first on PATH, e.g. `uv venv V && uv pip install --python V/bin/python klayout-tools==0.8.0 klayout==0.30.12 && PATH=V/bin:$PATH layout/output_stage/regen.sh`) |
| PDK | `gf180mcuD`, `open_pdks f6eeac7dad085ffcc829ccfd721f7b4ce39edcf7` (`~/.ciel/gf180mcuD`) |
| klayout python / klt's engine | `0.30.12`, pinned explicitly (`PILOT_KLAYOUT_PIN`); `tools/output_stage.py` refuses the run on a mismatch |

Nothing is installed on the host; no ngspice is run.

**Reproducibility, as measured at the pin above (klt 0.8.0 / klayout
0.30.12): regenerations are byte-identical.** Two consecutive runs of
`regen.sh` from a clean checkout produce the same composed-GDS sha256
(`3af96ba5689153a392aa50ac87eafb0c7e6978ad7dbe3ad9453d6e6b394ba151`) and a
byte-identical `results.json` (verified with `cmp` while preparing this
README). The one moving field across runs is
`evidence/output_stage.extract.klt.json`'s `timing.*` wall-clock block —
scheduler noise, not content. `tools/output_stage.py` asserts the tamper
check — "the checked layout is the layout this run produced, not an edited
one" — against **both** envelopes: the DRC envelope's
`provenance.input.content_hash` (`sha256:` + the GDS sha256, `role:
"layout"`) and the LVS envelope's `environment.layout_sha256` (the same
digest without the prefix), and the negative control's layout hash is
asserted equal to the positive run's.

## Results (actual runs; `results.json` and `evidence/` hold the raw output)

| Check | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Routed composition | `output_stage` cell, bbox 140.64 × 21.11 µm (not an area claim), **`unrouted_nets: []`**, 6 nets routed, 5 pin labels |
| DRC, klt curated deck | **clean** (0 violations), **80 rules checked / 34 skipped**, deck `sha256:0eb6df1bafc5106ec50bd58479dbd7cda36bb685f1ef18cd3a2244a6e7e48666` |
| Extract (`poly_res=1k`) | 14 nfet + 24 pfet (all 38 MOS fingers of XM6/XM7/XMB1 recognised) + 1 MIM + 1 ppolyf |
| LVS vs the cut sub-netlist | **match, `error_count: 0`** — 6/6 nets, 5/5 devices (`combine_devices` folds the fingers), pins 6 vs 5, matched 6 |
| Negative control | XCC `c_width` 16.8u → 20u in the reference only: **mismatch with 1 `device.property` error**, same layout sha256 as the positive |

Deck coverage, quoted from the committed envelope
(`evidence/output_stage.drc.klt.json` → `coverage`): 80 rules checked, 34
skipped, and `layers_in_stream_without_rules` = `34/10, 36/10, 42/10, 46/10,
81/10` — exactly the five *label* purpose layers the compose step writes
(`Metal1..Metal5` LABEL datatypes), which carry no geometry and so have no
rules. "DRC, klt curated" is klt's curated `gf180mcu` deck only (a subset of
the foundry rules; antenna and density are not covered), so `clean` is not
foundry DRC signoff (#282 keeps the PDK runset unavailable on these
workers).

The six `mismatch_count` entries in the LVS envelope are all
**warning-severity**, zero errors: two `device.geometry_not_compared` (the
MIM's `P` and the resistor's `A`/`P` are KLayout-*secondary* parameters,
never compared), one `device.parameter_excluded` (resistor `R` excluded by
the `L,W` compare scope), two `device.placeholder_value` (the subckt-call
reference form fills `C`/`R` with literal placeholders — `A`/`L`/`W`, the
geometry-derived parameters, are what is compared), and one `topology`
entry for the unused `bjt` class. The negative control above is the proof
the compared MIM parameters are live: a wrong plate width fails with
`device.property`.

**The resistor's substrate terminal is verified tied to `vss`:** the
extractor reports `X$40 vout nz vss ppolyf_u_1k` — the third terminal lands
on the same extracted net as the nfet substrate taps, and the LVS match is
against a reference whose XRZ card says `vss` there (`extracted/` holds the
netlist; no label or metal was hand-added for this — the substrate merges
through the p-substrate).

**Which pin-label claims are mechanical:** the compose response's `pins[]`
(all five `labelled: true`) and `ports[]` are asserted in `results.json` →
`routed.pins_labelled` = `n2, vout, ibias, vdd, vss`. Where each label
physically sits in the GDS: `vdd` at m6's `TAP_N` ring tap and `vss` at m7's
`TAP_W` ring tap (port-side labels), while `n2`, `vout` and `ibias` are
labelled by the router's own net-name label on their route planes —
gen-compose deduplicates a `pins[]` label at a routing terminal against
that route label rather than drawing it twice. Extraction sees all five
nets (plus `nz`, whose route label makes it a sixth extracted pin; the
reference keeps it internal and the comparer matches all pins —
`counts.pins: layout 6, reference 5, matched 6`). Guard rings/taps come
from the generators' own geometry (`add_guard_ring: true` per block, the
pilot verified ring/tap drawing and bulk-tie behaviour per cell) plus
inspection; `klt ring-check`/`klt tap-distance` are not expressible against
a composed cell (see `layout/first_stage/README.md`) and are not claimed as
a pass. `vdd` is routed from XM6's source to its own well ring (S and B are
both `vdd` on the card); `vss` ties both nfet sources to their substrate
tap rings.

## Files

- `regen.sh`, `tools/output_stage.py`: the recipe (imports the pilot's
  `schematic_cards` / `num_um` / `check_engine` / `control_tripped` /
  `layout_sha256_matches` — not forked)
- `gds/`: the five generated blocks + the composed, routed, labelled
  `output_stage.gds`
- `ref/`: the schematic cut (five cards verbatim) + the negative control's
  perturbed copy
- `extracted/`: the composed cell's extracted netlist
- `evidence/`: every klt envelope (gen ×5, placed + routed compose
  requests/responses, deck rules, DRC, extract, LVS, negative control) —
  the raw output the table above quotes
- `results.json`: the summary the driver asserts and the README quotes
